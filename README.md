General Information

This project focuses on understanding the demand for shared bikes in the US market after the COVID‑19 pandemic.
BoomBikes, a bike‑sharing company, faced revenue drops during lockdown and wants to plan ahead for recovery.
The business problem is to identify which factors (like weather, season, year, etc.) influence bike demand, so the company can adjust supply and strategy.
The dataset used contains daily bike rental counts along with variables such as temperature, humidity, season, weather situation, holidays, and year.

Conclusions

Bike demand is strongly influenced by temperature — warmer days see more rentals.
Season plays a big role: demand is higher in summer and fall compared to winter.
Weather conditions matter: clear days drive demand, while misty or rainy days reduce it.
Demand has been increasing year over year, showing that bike‑sharing is gaining popularity.
